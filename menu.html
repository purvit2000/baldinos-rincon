Our Menu

Every cold sub is served the Baldinos Way — lettuce, onion, tomatoes, vinegar, oil & seasonings. Sizes: Half, Whole, and Family. Any sandwich can be made as a wrap on request.

Make it your way

Choose your size

Half: 6½ inches. Whole: 13 inches. Family: the equivalent of three half subs. Family sizes are available where listed.

Bread & wraps

Choose Italian white or whole wheat bread. Any sandwich can be made as a wrap on request; call the shop for wrap sizing and pricing.

What comes on a cold sub?

The Baldinos Way includes lettuce, onion, tomato, oil, vinegar, and seasoning. Ask for mayo, mustard, hot peppers, or sweet peppers.

Allergies & dietary questions

Please call (912) 295-5184 before ordering to discuss ingredients and preparation. Ask about the Vegetarian Stir-Fry and available substitutions.
